Eligibility Criteria

  • • Primary School (Grades 1-5): Age 5-10 years
  • • Middle School (Grades 6-8): Age 11-13 years
  • • High School (Grades 9-12): Age 14-17 years
  • • Previous academic records required
  • • Transfer certificate from previous school

Fee Structure

  • • Primary School: $5,000 - $8,000/year
  • • Middle School: $8,000 - $12,000/year
  • • High School: $12,000 - $18,000/year
